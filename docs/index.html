About

Engineering experience
meets AI ambition

Mefford Innovations LLC is an independent software company with a singular focus: building AI tools that are genuinely useful. Not general-purpose chatbots, not gimmicks — purpose-driven applications engineered around specific problems, with a credit-based model that makes the value transparent.

Founded by Paul Mefford — a software engineer with 25+ years of experience across startups, enterprise corporations, and everything in between. His career spans companies like USANA Health Sciences, where he led a modern microservices migration, to early-stage startups where he grew infrastructure from a single environment to five AWS deployments serving 300+ clients.

An MBA from Western Governors University bridges the tech-business gap: every solution is evaluated not just on technical quality, but on whether it actually moves the needle for the people using it.
