FAQ

Common questions

How much does custom work cost?
It depends on the scope of the work — we'll talk through it together and put together a proposal before anything is built.
How long does it take?
It depends on the scope. Once we've talked through what you need, we'll give you a timeline as part of the proposal.

Ownership terms are part of the proposal we put together for your specific engagement — we'll cover it directly when we talk.

Yes. We explain everything in plain language — you don't need to understand the technology to work with us.
Do you work with businesses outside Utah County?
Utah County is home base, and we work with businesses beyond it as well.
What if it doesn't work?
We test against how your business actually runs before calling anything done, and we stay reachable after launch to work through issues as they come up.
