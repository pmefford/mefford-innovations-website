The problem

Business doesn't stop at 5pm — but your front desk does

Messages and inquiries arrive around the clock: a text after close, a web-chat question during a busy shift, a missed call from someone ready to book. Every one of those is a potential customer, and every one that goes unanswered is a chance a competitor answers first.

What it does

Answers, gathers the basics, hands it off

Answers web chat & texts, 24/7

Whenever a visitor or customer reaches out, day or night, something responds right away.

Gathers the basics

Name, what they need, preferred time, and contact info — the information your team needs to follow up.

Ready-to-confirm handoff

Your team reviews and approves with one click — you stay in control of every booking.

What it will never do

It doesn't give advice on serious or sensitive matters, and it doesn't try to be your whole front desk. Anything urgent or outside the ordinary gets routed straight to a person — it's built to know its limits.

It works alongside the tools you already use. Nothing to rip out, nothing to migrate.

Who it's for

Built for appointment-driven businesses

Home services, salons and studios, auto shops, fitness and wellness, and offices such as dental or chiropractic — any business where a missed after-hours message can mean a lost appointment.

Worth thinking about

What's a missed lead worth to you?

Think about your average appointment value and how many after-hours messages you get in a typical month — that's roughly what's on the table every time one goes unanswered. We can help you work out the real number for your business.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it replace my staff?
No. It covers the hours nobody's available and hands your team a ready-to-confirm item — your staff stays in control of every booking.
What if it gets something wrong?
Nothing is booked automatically — your team confirms every handoff, so mistakes get caught before they matter. Anything urgent or out of scope routes to a person.
Will it work with my calendar and software?
It's designed to work alongside the tools you already use. We'll confirm the specifics for your setup during our first conversation.
How long does setup take?
It depends on your setup and how it needs to connect to your existing tools — we'll give you a timeline after we talk through the details.
Who can see my customers' information?
Good question, and worth a direct conversation — get in touch and we'll walk through exactly how information is handled for your setup.
What does it cost to try?
It depends on the scope — we'll talk through it together. We're currently onboarding a small number of early partners at a discounted or pilot rate in exchange for feedback.
